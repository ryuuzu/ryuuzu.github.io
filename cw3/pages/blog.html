Technology: A Daily Driver?

Technology has become a daily driver in our lives. On the Review42 blog, Metev (2020) states that the average time spent on smartphones is 2hrs 51 mins a day, i.e. 171 mins per day (Metev, 2020). And this is just the average data, this doesn't count in the hours of time teenagers spend on social media apps like Tiktok, Instagram, Snapchat. Technology has become a must for many people these days. It not only helps them keep entertained but it also helps them be upto date with all the global news.

We all know that the world is still trying to recover from the global pandemic, Corona Virus, which affected people very badly and many companies had adapted to Work From Home. With this, there was a great increase in the laptop shipment worldwide. The total laptop shipment was 173 million units worldwide in 2019 (Alsop, 2021). But in 2020, the total laptop shipment was 218 million units. That was an increase of 45 million units, almost 27%, than last year. This was one of the highest increase in the history. So, it could be considered as the result of the global pandemic as well. The pandemic has made us aware about how important techonology can be when needed and used in right way. If the technology wasn't as advanced as now, this global pandemic could have been the biggest threat to the humans just like meteorites to dinosaurs. Everything could go to extinct. We could be getting off-track here let's get back to the technological facts.

We don't depend on the technology only for work. We can also get the latest updates with it. An article by Majid (2020) states that The Epoch Times, a pro-Trump right-wing title, had a growth of 421% since December 2019 from 10.2m to 53.4m visits per month.

Life isn't all about news and work. Entertainment also plays a big part in it. One of the biggest apps these days is, of course, TikTok. Tiktok is one of the fastest growing social media platforms in the work which presents an alternative version of online sharing. Tiktok has 689 million monthly active users worldwide (Mohsin, 2021). It is not only about social media. People also play various games on their devices. At the time of writing this blog, the SteamDB's data show that the concurrent online steam users are 24,813,163 (SteamDB, 2021).

In a nutshell, technology has become an important aspect of our daily life. It's not only about the handheld devices. Even TVs, fridges, cars, washing machines, etc. are getting smarter with the advancement of the technology. The future of these technologies can go even farther then the current level. All we can do is sit back and watch what the researchers and developers can do or become one of them and become a part of this great journey.
